02The instrument: proved broken, then proved fixed

Two rollout-independent tests arbitrate policy-vs-harness. The teacher-forced probe (above) showed the mapping was learned. The ground-truth replay (replay_diag2.py, byte-faithful world) showed the expert's own actions failed through the eval harness: miss 102–152 mm, nothing welded, the object towed across the room with zero drone contacts. Five defects, each alone sufficient for 0/20 — stale arm gate, air-weld, start distribution (0/225 training overlap), action clip, contact flicker. Repair (fixfit5.py, five gated iterations) was accepted only when replay passed: 3/3 placed, 7–13 mm from bin centre, weld within 4 ticks of the expert's — with both the expert arm and the deployable automaton. Fix flags: --platfix --startfix.

05Localising the residual miss

Spawn correlation — powered_corr.py, n=66

Miss vs the object's Mahalanobis distance from the training prior: radial r = +0.44 (mode-demeaned; significance ≈ 0.24). Directional: x-axis slope −0.86 (r −0.81), y-axis slope +0.02. Later refined by the audit: final jaw x tracks object x at slope +0.40 — partial tracking, not blindness; the policy closes ~40% of the lateral offset.

Instrumented audit — audit_analysis.py, n=20 tagged, jaws-frame

Jaws median miss 223 mm (body 365 mm — the 142 mm gap is the arm offset). Body-frame decomposition: lateral scatter (sd 263 mm, side split 55/45 — the observed "left" was sampling) plus a systematic 120 mm fore–aft undershoot (18/20). Heading: object inside the nose camera's ±65° FOV for 100% of every approach; nose-on-bearing slope +0.39; the heading-error sign test is non-discriminating (prior-following predicts the same 18/20 opposition).

The sentence the evening earned: pinning the commanded heading at the true bearing — changing nothing else — collapsed the median miss from 223 mm to 27–41 mm across two seeded replicates and produced the only pick-ups of the project. The aiming channel owns the residual failure; the last 30–40 mm is translational precision. The success-count swing between replicates (5 vs 1 of 20, paired miss diff +7 mm) is why the distance distribution, not the success rate, is the primary metric.
